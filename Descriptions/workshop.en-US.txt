A library for creating achievements in Terraria mods. Supports user, character, world and character-in-world progress, multiplayer and the native achievement menu.

[h2]Compatible mod[/h2]
[b]Banner Progress[/b] — Bestiary creature progression and four achievements with shared world goals.
